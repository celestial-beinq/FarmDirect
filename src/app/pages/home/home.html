Why we exist

Nigerians deserve to know what they are eating

Fruit ripened overnight with carbide. Beans sprayed with insecticide to keep weevils away. Palm oil coloured with dye. Food that passes through so many hands that nobody can say where it came from, or what seed it grew from. We started FarmDirect to give families a way to buy real food straight from farms that have been checked.
